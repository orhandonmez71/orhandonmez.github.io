MUCUR GRMHD codes

General-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics codes for accretion flows and jets around black holes. You can use them to solve your problem in 2D, in the meridional plane or in the equatorial plane, or in full 3D.

  • Fortran 90 · MPI · PARAMESH
  • Ideal GRMHD around Kerr black holes
  • 2D meridional · 2D equatorial · 3D
  • New problems solved in collaboration

2D Meridional plane

Flows in the (r, θ) plane from pole to pole: jets and polar outflows, accretion along the spin axis, spherical inflow, magnetised black holes and the Blandford–Znajek process. Validated against the Blandford–Znajek power.

2D Equatorial plane

Flows in the (r, φ) plane at θ = π/2: wind accretion and disks in the orbital plane, with non-axisymmetric structure.

Use the codes for your own problem

The source codes are not publicly distributed. They are tested and work reliably, and we are happy to solve new astrophysical problems with them in collaboration. We prepare the set-up for your problem (the header file and the initial data), you run the code, and we analyse and publish the results together.
