MUCUR GRMHD codes

General-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics codes for accretion flows and jets around black holes. You can use them to solve your problem in 2D, in the meridional plane or in the equatorial plane, or in full 3D, around Kerr black holes or in more than 60 black-hole space-times from modified gravity and other alternative theories.

2D · axisymmetric · release v4r

2D Meridional plane

Flows in the (r, θ) plane from pole to pole: jets and polar outflows, accretion along the spin axis, spherical inflow, magnetised black holes and the Blandford–Znajek process. Kerr and 70 modified-gravity space-times. Validated against the Blandford–Znajek power, the exact Michel solution, Bondi inflow onto a Kerr hole, Bondi–Hoyle–Lyttleton accretion and a Kerr hole in a uniform magnetic field (Wald).

See details → 2D · equatorial · release v24a

2D Equatorial plane

Flows in the (r, φ) plane at θ = π/2, all the way around the hole: wind accretion, shock cones and their oscillations (flip-flop, QPOs), spirals, rotating discs and tori. Kerr and 70 modified-gravity space-times. Five test problems with exact or published solutions; the magnetised Michel test passed with second-order convergence.

The developer

Prof. Dr. Orhan Dönmez is Professor of Physics at the American University of the Middle East in Kuwait. He received his PhD from Drexel University in 2002, with doctoral research at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center on general-relativistic hydrodynamics with adaptive-mesh refinement, and was a researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute). For more than two decades he has developed codes for relativistic flows around black holes and used them to study accretion, shock cones and quasi-periodic oscillations in general relativity and in alternative theories of gravity.

Not only Kerr: test the theory of gravity

Besides the Kerr metric of general relativity, the codes can evolve accretion flows and jets in more than 60 black-hole space-times: modified theories of gravity (for example Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet, Horndeski, Hořava, f(R, T), bumblebee and Kalb–Ramond gravity), quantum-corrected and regular black holes, black holes in nonlinear electrodynamics, and black holes surrounded by dark matter. With the same set-up and the same diagnostics, the effect of the theory of gravity on the flow can be compared directly with Kerr.

Use the codes for your own problem

The source codes are not publicly distributed. They are tested and work reliably, and we are happy to solve new astrophysical problems with them in collaboration. We prepare the set-up for your problem (the header file and the initial data), you run the code, and we analyse and publish the results together.
